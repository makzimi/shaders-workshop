# Attribution and asset status

This repository contains the live-coding sample prepared for Maxim Kachinkin's DevFest Baku shader workshop. The product UI, Upside Down implementation, images, video, icons and Suisse Intl fonts are retained from the supplied Drinkit shader sample. The workshop adds the spotlight exercise, independent Start/Finish exercises and teaching scaffolding.

The original asset hashes are retained in `asset-provenance.json`. Its reference paths describe the source archive and are not build dependencies. The original local-capture scope in that historical manifest is preserved; Max authorized this public workshop repository on 2026-10-18.

No project-wide open-source license or additional license to the branding, media or fonts is granted by this repository. Those materials remain subject to their respective owners' rights. Third-party dependencies retain their own licenses; dependencies are declared in `app/build.gradle.kts`. Gradle wrapper files retain their original copyright and license notices.
